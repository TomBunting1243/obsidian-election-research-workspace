# Project History

This chronology separates the original private-vault development from the later public-template extraction. The first two dates are based on the author's direct account of work completed with a different AI agent before the system was imported into the current workspace.

## July 9, 2026 — Concept and Michigan prototype

The project began as a Michigan election-research experiment. The initial work established the idea of one race note backed by structured source data and an interactive Obsidian module. It was developed with a different AI agent and later imported into the current system.

## July 20, 2026 — General election workspace

The Michigan approach was generalized into a broader workspace model: canonical race identifiers, normalized sidecars, generated-versus-handwritten boundaries, and repeatable race-note structure.

## August 23, 2026 — Reusable race dashboard

The common Dataview renderer was expanded so Senate, governor, and House notes could share one interactive module rather than maintaining a Michigan-only interface.

## August 25, 2026 — Broader source connectors

The private workspace added further polling, finance, and quantitative-model connectors while preserving one renderer contract.

## August 28, 2026 — Public starter-vault extraction

The reusable interface was separated from the private Jack vault, given configurable paths, paired with fictional fixtures, documented, tested for private-path leakage, and published as an installable starter vault.

The public repository's Git dates describe this extraction and its subsequent review work. This chronology records the earlier product development without presenting reconstructed commits as contemporaneous Git activity.
